Strength = how loose the joints are (over 100 = extra loose). Bounce = overshoot before settling. Wobble = soft, jelly-like jiggle. Sway = the body leans and swings when speeding up, stopping or turning. Weight = heavier limbs that sag and carry momentum. Ragdoll = active ragdoll: loose and floppy, and goes limp on trips and falls. Legs off keeps the feet planted.
